# TAWSILI — PREMIUM LOGISTICS WEBSITE & APPLICATION DESIGN SYSTEM

**Product:** Tawsili — توصيلي  
**Product Type:** Logistics & Shipment Management Platform  
**Stack:** Laravel 13, Blade, Tailwind CSS 4, Vite, Filament  
**Primary Language:** Arabic (RTL)  
**Secondary Language:** English (LTR)  
**Design Reference:** Supplied Transfox logistics website screenshot  
**Brand Identity:** Premium Red & White Corporate Logistics  
**Design Direction:** Editorial Corporate Logistics + Realistic 3D + Structured Visual Composition

---

# 1. DESIGN VISION

## 1.1 Overall Vision

Transform Tawsili into a premium logistics platform with a visual direction inspired by the supplied international logistics website reference.

The target design should follow the same visual character, layout composition, and level of visual sophistication:

- Professional corporate logistics identity.
- White navigation and header.
- Large cinematic dark hero section.
- Realistic three-dimensional delivery vehicle imagery.
- Strong white typography over dark backgrounds.
- Bold red geometric accents.
- Large photographic service sections.
- Editorial split layouts.
- Overlapping visual compositions.
- Clean statistics sections.
- Distinctive red and dark promotional sections.
- Professional contact and footer layouts.

The design must feel like a real international logistics company's website, adapted specifically to the actual Tawsili platform.

**Important:** Use the supplied screenshot as the primary visual reference for composition and aesthetic direction. Do not copy its logo, company name, exact assets, or copyrighted visual materials.

## 1.2 Design Personality

The interface must communicate:

- Reliability.
- Speed.
- Professionalism.
- Organization.
- Trust.
- Operational efficiency.
- Modern delivery services.

The result must not look like a generic AI-generated landing page or a basic Laravel template.

## 1.3 Scope and Protected Interfaces

This design system governs the Tawsili public logistics website and the existing customer and operations interfaces. It must not be used to restyle, restructure, or replace the AI assistant's design.

The AI assistant's views, styles, assets, interactions, and placement are protected from this visual implementation. Keep homepage-specific styles scoped to the homepage, and do not add global selectors that alter the assistant. Any future assistant redesign requires a separate, explicit request.

# 10. INTERACTION AND ANIMATION

- Button hover transitions.
- Navigation hover states.
- Smooth dropdowns.
- Subtle image movement.
- Refined card hover.
- Section reveal effects.
- Shipment timeline transitions.
- Small feedback animations.

Timing guidelines:

- Button: 150–200ms.
- Navigation: 150–250ms.
- Card hover: 200–300ms.
- Modal: 200–400ms.
